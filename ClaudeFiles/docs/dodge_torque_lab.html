Reading the model

  • The launch direction comes from the CM-to-ball line, not the contact normal. RocketSim builds the extra impulse from the vector between the car's center of mass and the ball's center, squashes its vertical part to 35%, and removes 35% of its forward component. At the default 45° with the center of mass on the line, that impulse points about 28° above horizontal, not 45°.
  • Spin only matters if contact lasts. The dodge torque ramps the car to 5.5 rad/s in about three ticks (25 ms). At the defaults the extra impulse separates the ball after two ticks, so the spin barely changes the result. In geometries where the car keeps pushing it matters a lot: at tilt −45°, front flip, offset at the nose end, the spin raises ball speed from about 540 to 1220 uu/s.
  • Ball spin comes from friction on the sliding face. With the car's 500 uu/s kick sliding along a tilted underside, the high contact friction (2.0) grips and spins the ball at about 2.8 rad/s even with the torque switched off. Set friction to 0 and the spin goes to zero.
  • Moving the contact point shifts the result predictably. At the default setup, sliding the contact from the tail end to the nose end of the underside (back flip) drops ball speed from about 680 to 455 uu/s and lifts the launch angle from 22° to 47°.
  • Repeated extra impulses compound. RocketSim reapplies the extra impulse every second tick while contact persists, using the current relative speed. A ball held against a moving face can gain speed geometrically. That is the rule as written in Arena.cpp, and this page follows it.

Model and limits

Planar (side view, no roll or yaw dodges), no gravity (both bodies fall together), ball at rest before contact unless closing speed is set. Contact is solved per 120 Hz tick with sequential impulses, friction cone 2.0, restitution 0, like RocketSim's car-ball override. Octane hitbox 120.5 × 38.7 uu offset (13.9, 20.8) from the center of mass, car mass 180, ball mass 30 with I = 0.4·m·r², car inertia from the box. Extra impulse follows Arena::_BtCallback_OnCarBallCollision; dodge constants follow Car::_UpdateAirTorque and RLConst.h. This is a re-implementation, not RocketSim itself, and has not been checked against RocketSim runs because the collision meshes are not in the repo. Treat sizes of effects as estimates and directions as reliable.
